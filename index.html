Read the evidence, preserve the question.

FEL tests pervasive site selection across the tested branches. Purifying and diversifying evidence receive equal visual weight.

MEME tests episodic diversification at a site. It does not provide evidence for purifying selection or by itself establish a specific selected branch.

Contrast-FEL compares branch groups. A difference does not establish positive selection or pinpoint when a shift occurred. Available here only as a synthetic illustration.

Nonsignificance is not proof of neutrality. These are inferred selective pressures, not a measured fitness landscape or a new statistical test. Significant sites and branches from different methods must not be combined to infer selected branch–site intersections.
